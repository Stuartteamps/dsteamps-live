What it is not

It is not a promise of off-market inventory. Private opportunities exist only when a seller chooses that route, and they are shared only as the seller allows.

It is not a newsletter. Texts are sent only if you tick the box above, and you can stop them at any time by replying STOP.

Questions

Frequently asked

Are there really homes that never reach the open market?

Sometimes. Some sellers prefer to sell quietly, and their homes are shared only as they allow. That is not a guaranteed supply, so Dan will also tell you about homes on the MLS that fit what you asked for.

Do I have to agree to texts?

No. The texting box is optional and unchecked. Leave it empty and Dan will call or email instead. If you do agree, you can reply STOP at any time.

Do I need to be ready to buy now?

No. Choose the timeline that fits and Dan will pace what he sends accordingly.
