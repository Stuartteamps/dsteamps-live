02The Homes

A desert house is built around the light.

Deep overhangs. Glass that faces the mountain. Shadow lines that move across the wall all afternoon. The good ones were designed for this place, and you can feel it when you walk in.

Before you write an offer

The land

Some desert properties sit on land the owner holds outright and some on leased land. The terms differ property by property and change how a lender sees the file. The answer is in that property's own documents.

The envelope

Roof, glazing, mechanical systems, anything added to the original footprint. Desert sun is hard on a building, and the permits tell you whether the work was done right.

The paperwork

What's recorded, what's disclosed, and what any association actually controls. Read it before you fall in love with the house.

Frequently asked

How do I get a valuation on my Palm Springs home?

Send the address to Dan Stuart at 619-677-0900 or stuartteamps@gmail.com. He prepares a written valuation for that specific property and shows you the comparable sales behind the number.

Which areas does the Stuart Team cover?

Palm Springs, Rancho Mirage, Palm Desert, Cathedral City, La Quinta, Indian Wells and Desert Hot Springs, across the Coachella Valley.

Can I work with you from outside the United States?

Yes. Dan previews properties, sends walkthrough video of the specific rooms you ask about, and coordinates with your attorney, lender and tax advisor. Requirements for closing in person depend on your lender and escrow company and are confirmed in writing early.

What is the difference between owned and leased land in Palm Springs?

Some properties sit on land the owner holds outright, others on leased land, and the terms differ property by property. It affects financing, so confirm it from that property's own title documents before making an offer.
