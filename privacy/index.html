Privacy Policy

This page explains, in plain English, what information this website collects, why, who sees it, and the choices you have. If anything here is unclear, call or email Dan and he will answer you directly.

Last updated: 2026-11-23

Who we are

This website, www.dsteamps.com, is run by Dan Stuart of the Stuart Team Real Estate Group, a real estate agent with Coldwell Banker Realty (California DRE #02043742), 1081 N. Palm Canyon Dr, Palm Springs, CA 92262. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean Dan Stuart and the Stuart Team.

What we collect

What you type into a form. When you ask for a home value, the relocation guide, the private list, a showing or an open house sign-in, we receive what you enter: usually your name, email address and phone number, and depending on the form, a property address, your timeline, a price range, the areas you are interested in, whether you already work with an agent, and any message you write.

How you found us. With a form we also receive the page you sent it from and, if you arrived through a tagged link (for example from Instagram or an email), the campaign tags in that link, such as utm_source=instagram. These tags describe the link, not you.

Website analytics, only if you accept. If you click Accept on the cookie notice, Google Analytics records how the site is used: pages viewed, clicks on the call, text and email buttons, when a form is started or sent, your device and browser type, the site that referred you, and an approximate location (about city level) that Google works out from your internet address. If you click Decline, or never choose, none of this is collected.

We never send your name, email address, phone number, property address or message to Google Analytics or any other analytics service. Analytics only ever sees the type of form and the campaign tags described above.

How we use it

To answer you: to prepare the home value review, send the guide, arrange the showing or follow up on the request you made.

To work with you as a client if you choose to, and to keep the records a California real estate agent and brokerage are required to keep.

To send you the emails or texts you asked for. Every email lets you unsubscribe, and you can reply STOP to any text.

To understand, in aggregate, which pages and campaigns are useful, so the website can be improved. This uses analytics only if you accepted it.

Who we share it with

Coldwell Banker Realty, the brokerage Dan works under, because client records belong to the brokerage as well as the agent.

Service providers that run this website for us and may handle your information only to provide their service: GitHub Pages (hosts the website), Vercel (receives form submissions), Resend (delivers the form to Dan's inbox by email), Google (Tag Manager and Analytics, only if you accepted), Coldwell Banker Home (the CRM pixel, only if you accepted), and the email and client-management tools Dan uses as a Coldwell Banker agent.

Other people in a real estate transaction, such as a lender, escrow or title company, only when you ask us to or the transaction requires it.

Anyone we are legally required to share it with, for example in response to a valid court order.

Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with anyone for advertising that follows you across other websites. This website runs no advertising or remarketing tags.

You can still opt out at any time, and we will keep honoring it if that ever changes: use the button below. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, we treat it as the same opt-out automatically.

Analytics stay off until you accept.

Cookies and similar technology

Your Accept or Decline choice is saved in your own browser so we do not ask again on every page.

Campaign tags from the link you arrived on are kept in your browser for that visit only, so a form you send a few pages later still shows how you found the site.

Google Analytics sets its cookies only after you click Accept. You can change your mind at any time with the Cookie settings button at the bottom of every page, or by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Your privacy rights

If you live in California, you have the right to know what personal information we have about you and how we got it, to get a copy, to have it corrected, to have it deleted, and to opt out of its sale or sharing. We will not treat you differently or charge you more for using any of these rights.

We do not collect sensitive personal information, such as Social Security, bank or health details, through this website, and we do not use personal information to make automated decisions about you.

We will honor the same requests from anyone, wherever you live.

How to make a request

Email stuartteamps@gmail.com or call 619-677-0900 and say what you would like: a copy, a correction, deletion, or an opt-out.

To protect you, we will confirm the request comes from you by checking it against the contact details we already have. Someone you authorize may make a request for you with your written permission.

We will respond within 45 days. Some records must be kept even after a deletion request, for example transaction files that California real estate law requires us to keep; if that applies, we will tell you which records and why.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while we are working with you or you are still receiving updates you asked for, and afterwards only as long as the law or the brokerage's record-keeping rules require. Analytics data is kept by Google under the retention period set in our Google Analytics account.

Security

The website and its forms use encrypted connections (HTTPS). Form submissions go directly to Dan's inbox and are not published anywhere. No system is perfectly secure, so please do not send Social Security, bank or other highly sensitive details through a website form.

Children

This website is for adults buying, selling or renting property. It is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle information, we will update this page and the date at the top. If a change is significant, such as adding advertising tags, we will say so here before it takes effect.

Contact

Questions about privacy go straight to Dan Stuart: stuartteamps@gmail.com or 619-677-0900, or by mail to 1081 N. Palm Canyon Dr, Palm Springs, CA 92262.
