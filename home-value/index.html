Why not an instant estimate

Automated estimates work from public records. They cannot see a remodel, a view, the condition of a roof or whether the land under a house is owned or leased. In Palm Springs, that last point alone can move value considerably, and it is invisible to an algorithm.

An estimate you cannot check is not much use when you are deciding whether to sell. A written analysis shows its working.

What happens next

Dan reviews the public record for the address, then contacts you to confirm a few details. Some homes he will ask to see; for others the record and a conversation are enough.

There is no obligation to list, and your details are not passed to anyone else.

Questions

Frequently asked

Is there a charge for the market analysis?

No. The analysis is free and there is no obligation to list your home with Dan Stuart or Coldwell Banker Realty.

Is this an instant online estimate?

No. Dan prepares the analysis himself from recent comparable sales, current competition and the specifics of your property, then explains the range and the reasoning behind it.

Does Dan need to see the house?

Not always. For many homes the public record and a short conversation are enough. If condition, updates or views are likely to move the number, Dan will ask to see it.

Which areas does this cover?

Palm Springs and the wider Coachella Valley, including Rancho Mirage, Palm Desert, Cathedral City, La Quinta, Indian Wells and Desert Hot Springs.
