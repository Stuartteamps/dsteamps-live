The home

A single-level 2004 contemporary in Palm Springs Colony, set behind oleander, bougainvillea and red bird of paradise on a planted corner lot. Two street frontages, and the garden still keeps to itself.

Inside, the great room rises to a stepped volume ceiling, and a multi-sided glass fireplace sits in the divide so the fire reads from both sides. The kitchen runs on a long island with slab stone counters, natural maple cabinetry and bar seating, with a built-in desk beside it.

The primary suite opens directly onto the pool deck through its own slider. A casita with its own bath opens off the rear terrace.

Outside, a raised stone-faced spa spills into the pool, seating terraces step out from the covered patio, and Mount San Jacinto closes the view. Fee land, no HOA. Leased solar is in place. Offered furnished, including the art.
