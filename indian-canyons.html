Indian Canyons — the character of the neighborhood

Indian Canyons is in south Palm Springs, against the mountains — wider streets, bigger lots and long mountain views, with golf woven through the area. It reads calmer and more spread out than the neighborhoods closer to downtown.

Descriptions of a neighborhood are a starting point, never a substitute for standing on the street at the hour you would actually be living there. Go at midday and again after dark.

What to look at in Indian Canyons specifically

Views and golf frontage are the things people buy here, so confirm in writing exactly what protects a view and what any association or club membership actually obliges you to. Land terms in south Palm Springs vary by property; check the title first.

Bring the question to the specific address. Two houses on the same block can carry completely different land terms, permit histories and association rules.

What to check before you write an offer

Land ownership first. Some desert properties sit on land the owner holds outright and some on land that is leased. The terms differ property by property, and they change how a lender looks at the file, so the only reliable answer comes from that property's own documents.

Then the envelope: roof, glazing, mechanical systems and anything that has been added to the original footprint. Desert sun is hard on a building, and permits tell you whether the work was done properly.

Then the paperwork around the house: what is recorded, what is disclosed, and what any governing association actually controls. Ask for the documents and read them before you fall in love.
