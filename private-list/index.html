What it is not

It is not a promise of off-market inventory. Most homes in the Coachella Valley are marketed through the MLS, and you will see those through the list too. Private opportunities exist only when a seller chooses that route, and they are shared only as the seller allows.

It is not a newsletter. You can stop it at any time by replying STOP or UNSUBSCRIBE.

Questions

Frequently asked

Will I get off-market homes?

Sometimes, and only when a seller allows it. Most homes are marketed through the MLS, so the list gives you early notice of new and private opportunities rather than a guaranteed supply of off-market homes.

How often will Dan send homes?

Only when something matches your budget, areas and timeline. There is no fixed schedule, and you can stop it at any time by replying STOP or UNSUBSCRIBE.

Do I need to be ready to buy now?

No. Many people join while they are still deciding. Choose the timeline that fits and Dan will pace what he sends accordingly.
